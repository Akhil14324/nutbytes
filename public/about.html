Our Story

A little bite of goodness, from Ongole.

NutBytes began with a simple idea: snacks can be delicious and wholesome at the same time. We make traditional sweets and treats with the finest nuts, seeds and dry fruits.

Every laddu, chikki, sunnunda and ariselu is made the traditional way, with no processed sugar and no maida. From festival boxes to everyday snacking, we want every bite to be a treat you can feel good about.
