Our Story

A little bite of goodness, from Ongole.

NutBytes began with a simple idea: snacks can be delicious and wholesome at the same time. We make traditional sweets and treats with the finest nuts, seeds and dry fruits.

Every laddu, chikki, sunnunda and ariselu is made the traditional way, with no processed sugar and no maida. From festival boxes to everyday snacking, we want every bite to be a treat you can feel good about.

Meet the Founder – Himabindu

Hello! I'm Himabindu, the founder of NutBytes, a passionate homemaker and a proud mother of two wonderful children.

My journey with NutBytes began with a simple thought – to provide healthy, tasty and nutritious snacks for my children.

As a mother, I always wanted my kids to enjoy snacks that are not only delicious but also made with care and goodness. This inspired me to experiment with different varieties of homemade snacks using wholesome ingredients.

As I explored and prepared these healthy treats, I realized that many other families might be looking for the same kind of nutritious and tasty snacking options. That's when a small idea turned into a big dream – NutBytes was born!

Through NutBytes, my mission is to bring healthier snacking choices to every family, combining taste, quality and care in every bite.

What started as a mother's love for her children has now become my passion to make healthy snacking a part of everyone's life.

NutBytes – Made with love, inspired by motherhood, and created for healthier snacking!

Meet the Co-Founder – Dr. Ajay Krishna

Behind every dream is a strong support system, and for me, that person has always been my husband.

A doctor by profession, he has been my constant source of encouragement, motivation and support throughout my journey with NutBytes. From believing in my idea to encouraging me to turn my passion into a business, his support has played a very important role in building NutBytes.

With his support and vision, we started packages suited for corporate gifting, wedding gifts, festival sharing and special occasion treats.

Together, we believe that healthy food can be both delicious and enjoyable. His encouragement and my passion for creating wholesome snacks have brought us together to build NutBytes with love, care and a shared commitment to healthier living.

NutBytes is not just a business for us; it's a family dream built on love, support and the desire to make a positive difference in people's lives.
