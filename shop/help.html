FAQs

Do your snacks contain sugar or maida?

Our snacks are made with no processed sugar and no maida.

Where do you deliver?

We deliver in Ongole and nearby areas. If you're further away, message us on WhatsApp and we'll let you know.

Are the prices on the website final?

We confirm the final price, and any delivery charge, with you on WhatsApp before we pack your order.

Can I order gift boxes for an event?

Yes. Message us on WhatsApp with the occasion, the number of boxes and the date, and we'll help you plan it.

Is NutBytes FSSAI registered?

Yes. Our FSSAI registration number is 20126171000742.

Order & Delivery

Add your snacks to the cart and check out with your delivery address. After you place the order, tap “Send Order on WhatsApp”. Your order is already typed in, so just check it and press send. We'll reply to confirm the price, any delivery charge and the delivery time.

You can also order directly on WhatsApp at +91 90002 42480.

Returns & Refunds

If something arrives damaged or isn't right, message us on WhatsApp with your order ID and a photo, and we'll sort it out with you.
